In the field of hematologic oncology, Rituximab is a classic targeted biologic with a long history of clinical use. Since its introduction, Rituximab has been widely used in the treatment of various B-cell-related hematologic malignancies and has become an important component of treatment regimens for certain types of non-Hodgkin lymphoma, follicular lymphoma, diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, and chronic lymphocytic leukemia.

As biosimilars have increasingly entered different markets, the supply of Rituximab is no longer limited to a single originator product. For hospitals, pharmacies, pharmaceutical distributors, and other qualified procurement organizations, originator Rituximab and different biosimilar options may be considered according to product requirements, procurement budgets, supply conditions, and the regulatory requirements of the destination market, provided that applicable regulations and clinical-use requirements are met.

## What Is Rituximab?

Rituximab is a monoclonal antibody targeting CD20. CD20 is primarily expressed on the surface of B lymphocytes. By binding to CD20, Rituximab promotes the depletion of relevant B cells and thereby produces its therapeutic effects.

With its well-established targeted mechanism and extensive clinical experience, Rituximab has become an important biologic in the treatment of B-cell-related diseases. It is used in a range of hematologic conditions, although specific indications and treatment regimens should be determined according to the approved labeling of each product and the judgment of qualified healthcare professionals.

For patients, Rituximab is a specialized medical treatment and should not be selected, dosed, or substituted based solely on information available online. For procurement organizations, it is also important to consider the product source, specifications, regulatory status, supply conditions, and applicable regulations in the destination country or region.

## Why Are Rituximab Biosimilars Available?

Rituximab is a complex biologic product. Unlike conventional chemically synthesized medicines, biologics are produced through complex biological systems. Therefore, biosimilars should not be viewed simply as conventional generic drugs.

Biosimilars undergo appropriate quality, analytical, non-clinical, and clinical evaluations to demonstrate a high degree of similarity to the reference biologic and to establish that there are no clinically meaningful differences.

As relevant patents and market protections for originator Rituximab have expired in various markets, different pharmaceutical companies have developed and introduced Rituximab biosimilars, resulting in a broader range of products within the Rituximab supply landscape.

Today, Rituximab biosimilars are available in multiple markets worldwide. Examples in certain markets include Truxima, Ruxience, and Riabni, which are Rituximab biosimilars. However, product approvals, indications, and product names may vary between countries and regions, so the regulatory status of a specific product should be verified for the intended market before procurement.

## What Is the Difference Between Originator Rituximab and Biosimilars?

For procurement organizations, originator products and biosimilars should not simply be viewed as "expensive" versus "less expensive." They represent different products and supply systems, and procurement decisions may also depend on local registration requirements, import policies, clinical-use requirements, and product availability.

Originator Rituximab has a long history of market use. Some hospitals, healthcare institutions, and procurement programs may have specific requirements for an originator brand. When a particular originator product is required, procurement should be based on the specific brand, strength or presentation, quantity, and destination market.

Biosimilars provide additional product options for procurement organizations. As market competition develops, some biosimilars may be available at different price levels compared with originator products, potentially providing healthcare institutions and other qualified buyers with additional budget options.

It is important to note that procurement prices are not fixed. Factors such as the manufacturer, product specifications, purchase quantity, destination, market registration status, logistics requirements, and current supply conditions may all affect the final procurement terms. Rather than relying solely on a publicly listed price, it is generally more practical to conduct product-specific quotation and supply matching based on the actual procurement requirements.

## Different Budget Options for Rituximab Procurement

For international pharmaceutical procurement, one important characteristic of Rituximab is the increasing diversity of available products. Depending on procurement requirements, buyers can consider originator products as well as biosimilars that meet applicable regulatory and procurement requirements.

For example, customers with specific brand requirements may prioritize the availability of an originator Rituximab product. Customers who are more focused on procurement costs may also explore biosimilar options that meet the relevant regulatory and procurement conditions.

Therefore, Rituximab procurement does not necessarily involve selecting a single product. Depending on the requirements of the buyer, different product options and corresponding budget solutions may be considered.

During the procurement process, the following information should generally be confirmed:
- Product name and whether an originator or biosimilar is required
- Product strength, dosage form, and packaging
- Planned purchase quantity
- Destination country or region
- Whether a specific brand is required
- Local registration and import requirements
- Batch information, expiry date, and quality documentation requirements
- Storage and transportation conditions

Providing this information can help determine suitable products and supply solutions more accurately.
