# Mechanism of Action of HLX3901

In recent years, cancer immunotherapy has evolved from the **single-target era** to a **multi-dimensional synergy era**.

HLX3901, a next-generation **tetraspecific antibody developed by Henlius**, represents a major innovation in antibody engineering. It simultaneously targets:

- Two epitopes of **DLL3**
- **CD3** (T cell activation)
- **CD28** (co-stimulation)

👉 This enables **precise tumor targeting + full T cell activation**

Traditional approaches:

HLX3901 goes further:
