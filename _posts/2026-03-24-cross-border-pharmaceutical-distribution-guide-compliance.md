## I. How Do Pharmaceuticals Safely “Leave China”?

Pharmaceutical logistics differs fundamentally from general cargo:

- **Innovative drugs / biologics** → typically air freight  
- **APIs** → often shipped by sea (cost-efficient)  
- **Conventional drugs** → flexible multimodal transport  

But the real core is not *how*, but 👇

### ❄️ Temperature Control Integrity

- Is **2–8°C (or required range)** maintained throughout?
- Is there a **complete temperature log**?
- Does packaging meet **international standards (GDP/GSP)**?

👉 In pharma logistics:  
**“Delivered” ≠ “Qualified delivery.”**

## II. Customs Declaration — The First Real Barrier

Many assume logistics providers handle everything.

In reality, **customs declaration is the first critical checkpoint.**

### Required Documents

**Basic:**
- Invoice  
- Packing List  
- Contract  

**Pharma-specific:**
- Drug registration / filing documents  
- COA (Certificate of Analysis)  
- Certificate of Origin  
- Batch release certificate (if required)

### Key Risk Points

- ❌ Document inconsistency  
- ❌ Incorrect HS code  
- ❌ Misclassification (e.g., controlled substances)

👉 Even minor errors can lead to **inspection, delay, or rejection**.

## III. Customs Clearance — The Real Deciding Step

![Pharmaceutical Warehouse & Distribution](/assets/images/pharma-warehouse-conveyor.webp)

After export, the real challenge often begins **in the destination country**.

Typical clearance process:

1. Document review  
2. Import approval  
3. Inspection / sampling  
4. Temperature data verification  

### Common Failure Points

- ❌ No local drug registration  
- ❌ Missing import license  
- ❌ Incomplete temperature records  
- ❌ Mismatch between goods and documents  

👉 Most failures occur **here—not during transport.**
