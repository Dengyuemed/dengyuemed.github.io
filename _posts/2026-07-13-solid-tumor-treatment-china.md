## China's Solid Tumor Treatment Landscape in 2026

China has one of the world's largest cancer patient populations. Continuous investment in oncology research, regulatory reform, and biotechnology innovation has transformed the country's cancer treatment landscape.

Three major trends define solid tumor treatment in China during 2026.

### 1. CAR-T Therapy Is Expanding into Solid Tumors

For many years, CAR-T therapy was mainly used for hematologic malignancies.

China is now leading global research into applying CAR-T therapy to solid tumors.

One of the most important milestones is **CT041 (Satri-cel)**, the world's first Claudin18.2 (CLDN18.2)-targeted CAR-T therapy for advanced gastric and gastroesophageal junction cancer.

Clinical studies have demonstrated encouraging improvements in progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS), offering new treatment opportunities for patients whose disease has progressed after conventional therapies.

Multiple leading Chinese cancer centers continue to participate in CAR-T clinical research for solid tumors.

---

### 2. Rapid Expansion of Immunotherapy and ADC Therapy

China has approved more than twenty PD-1 and PD-L1 inhibitors.

These medicines are now widely combined with:

- Chemotherapy
- Anti-angiogenic therapy
- Targeted therapy
- Radiotherapy

Antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) have also become one of the fastest-growing treatment areas.

Current research focuses on biomarkers including:

- HER2
- TROP2
- CLDN18.2
- Nectin-4

These therapies are expanding treatment options for patients with:

- Breast cancer
- Lung cancer
- Gastric cancer
- Urothelial carcinoma
- Other advanced solid tumors

---

### 3. Precision Medicine Continues to Advance

Comprehensive molecular testing has become increasingly routine before treatment decisions.

Common diagnostic techniques include:

- Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS)
- Liquid biopsy
- PD-L1 testing
- MSI-H / dMMR testing
- HER2 testing
- CLDN18.2 biomarker analysis

These tests help physicians identify patients who may benefit from personalized targeted therapies or immunotherapy.

China has also expanded access to minimally invasive interventional oncology, including:

- TACE
- Radiofrequency ablation
- Microwave ablation
- Radioactive seed implantation
- Proton therapy
- Heavy ion therapy
