# 38 Innovative Drugs Approved in China in the First Half of 2026

According to public information released on the website of China's National Medical Products Administration (NMPA), **38 innovative drugs** had been approved in China as of **July 14, 2026**.

Chinese pharmaceutical wholesaler **DengYueMed** has compiled an overview of these newly approved innovative drugs, summarizing their **Marketing Authorization Holders (MAHs)** and approved indications to provide professional market information that supports greater global accessibility of high-quality medicines from China.

---

## 1. Sonrotoclax Tablets

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** BeiGene, Ltd.

Approved under the **Priority Review and Conditional Approval** pathway for:

- Adult patients with **chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL)** or **small lymphocytic lymphoma (SLL)** who have received at least one prior systemic therapy including a **Bruton tyrosine kinase (BTK) inhibitor**.
- Adult patients with **relapsed or refractory mantle cell lymphoma (MCL)** who have received at least two prior systemic therapies, including a BTK inhibitor.

---

## 2. Plozasiran Sodium Injection

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

Indicated, in combination with dietary control, to reduce triglyceride levels in adult patients with **familial chylomicronemia syndrome (FCS)**.

---

## 3. Retlirafusp Alfa Injection

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Suzhou Suncadia Biopharmaceuticals Co., Ltd.

Indicated in combination with fluoropyrimidine- and platinum-based chemotherapy for the first-line treatment of patients with **PD-L1-positive (CPS ≥1)**, locally advanced unresectable, recurrent, or metastatic **gastric or gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma** confirmed by a validated diagnostic test.

---

## 4. Olorigliflozin Capsules

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Yi Chang HEC Chang Jiang Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd.

Indicated for improving glycemic control in adults with **type 2 diabetes mellitus**.

---

## 5. Libevitug Injection

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Huahui Health Ltd.

Approved under the **Priority Review and Conditional Approval** pathway for the treatment of adult patients with **chronic hepatitis D virus (HDV) infection**, with or without compensated cirrhosis.

---

## 6. Ecnoglutide Injection

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Hangzhou Sciwind Biosciences Co., Ltd.

Indicated for improving glycemic control in adults with **type 2 diabetes mellitus**.

---

## 7. Amdokitug Injection

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Sunshine Guojian Pharmaceutical (Shanghai) Co., Ltd.

Indicated for the treatment of **moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis** in adults who are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy.

---

## 8. Rovadicitinib Tablets

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Chia Tai Tianqing Pharmaceutical Group Co., Ltd.

Indicated as first-line treatment for adult patients with:

- Intermediate-2 or high-risk **primary myelofibrosis (PMF)**
- **Post-polycythemia vera myelofibrosis (PPV-MF)**
- **Post-essential thrombocythemia myelofibrosis (PET-MF)**

For the treatment of disease-related splenomegaly or disease-related symptoms.

---

## 9. Sosimerasib Tablets

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Zhejiang Hangyu Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd.

Approved under the **Priority Review and Conditional Approval** pathway for adult patients with advanced **non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC)** harboring a **KRAS G12C mutation** who have received at least one prior systemic therapy.

---

## 10. Recombinant Erythropoietin Stimulating Protein Injection

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Guangdong Sansheng Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd.

Indicated for the treatment of anemia due to **chronic kidney disease** in patients receiving hemodialysis who are undergoing erythropoiesis-stimulating agent therapy.

---

## 11. Depemokimab Injection

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** GlaxoSmithKline Trading Services Limited

Indicated as maintenance treatment for **severe eosinophilic asthma** in adults and adolescents aged 12 years and older.

---

## 12. Higenamine Hydrochloride Injection

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Zhuhai Rundu Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd.

Indicated as a pharmacologic stress agent for **myocardial perfusion imaging (MPI)** to evaluate myocardial ischemia.

---

## 13. 99mTc-3PRGD2 Injection

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Foshan Rui Diao Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd.

A radiopharmaceutical targeting **integrin αvβ3** for **SPECT/CT imaging**, indicated as an adjunctive diagnostic agent for assessing regional lymph node metastasis in patients with suspected lung cancer.

---

## 14. Sevabertinib Tablets

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Bayer HealthCare Pharmaceuticals Inc.

Approved under the **Priority Review and Conditional Approval** pathway as monotherapy for adult patients with unresectable locally advanced or metastatic **NSCLC** harboring activating **HER2 (ERBB2) mutations** who have received at least one prior systemic therapy.

---

## 15. Bezacitinib Tablets

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Hangzhou Bangshun Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd.

Indicated as first-line treatment for adult patients with:

- Intermediate-2 or high-risk PMF
- PPV-MF
- PET-MF

To treat disease-related splenomegaly or improve disease-related symptoms.

---

## 16. Andamertinib Capsules

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Avistone (Ningbo) Biotechnology Co., Ltd.

Approved under the **Priority Review and Conditional Approval** pathway for adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic **NSCLC** harboring **EGFR exon 20 insertion mutations** whose disease has progressed during or after platinum-based chemotherapy or who are intolerant to platinum-based chemotherapy.

---

## 17. Firsekibart Injection

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Changchun GeneScience Pharmaceuticals Co., Ltd.

Indicated for the treatment of **acute gout flares** in adults who have contraindications to, intolerance of, or inadequate response to NSAIDs and/or colchicine, and for whom repeated corticosteroid therapy is not appropriate.

---

## 18. Nipocalimab-aahu Injection

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Janssen-Cilag International NV

Indicated in combination with standard therapy for adult and adolescent patients (12 years and older) with **generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG)** who are anti-AChR antibody-positive or anti-MuSK antibody-positive.

---

## 19. Fazamorexant Tablets

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Jiangsu COAST PHARMACEUTICAL CO., LTD.

Indicated for the treatment of **insomnia** characterized by difficulty with sleep onset and/or sleep maintenance in adults.

---

## 20. Anbenitamab Injection

**Marketing Authorization Holder:** Shanghai JMT Biological Technology Co., Ltd.

Indicated in combination with chemotherapy for adults with locally advanced or metastatic **HER2-positive gastric or gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma** who have previously received at least one trastuzumab-containing regimen.
