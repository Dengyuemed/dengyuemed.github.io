## 1. What Types of Medicines Do Chinese Pharmaceutical Suppliers Offer?

Common categories include over-the-counter (OTC) medicines, prescription medicines, biological products, generic medicines, branded medicines and innovative drugs.

### 1.1 OTC Medicines

![Pharmacy shelves stocked with OTC medicines](/assets/images/pharmacy-shelves-otc-medicines.jpeg)

[Over-the-counter (OTC) medicines](https://dengyuerx.com/product-category/otc-pharmacy/) are products that can be purchased and used without a prescription in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.

Common categories include:
- Cold and related symptom relief medicines
- Antipyretic and analgesic medicines
- Gastrointestinal medicines
- Certain topical dermatological medicines
- Selected vitamin and mineral preparations

OTC medicines are widely used in retail pharmacy settings and everyday healthcare.

### 1.2 Prescription Medicines

[Prescription medicines](https://dengyuerx.com/product-category/prescription-medicines/) generally require a prescription issued by a qualified healthcare professional in accordance with local regulations and must be dispensed or used in line with applicable requirements.

They cover a wide range of therapeutic areas, including:
- Cardiovascular and metabolic diseases
- Anti-infective medicines
- Neurological disorders
- Endocrine disorders
- Oncology and hematological diseases

Prescription medicine supply involves more than product sourcing. It also requires attention to product qualifications, product documentation, batch information and cross-border distribution requirements.

### 1.3 Biological Products and Biosimilars

Biological products are manufactured using living organisms, cells or related biotechnological processes and include a range of complex therapeutic products. Biosimilars are biological products that are highly similar to an already approved reference biologic and meet applicable regulatory requirements.

Common product types include:
- Monoclonal antibodies and other therapeutic proteins
- Certain immunotherapy medicines
- Insulin and related biological products
- Other therapeutic biologics

Compared with many conventional chemical medicines, certain biological products have more specific requirements for storage temperatures, transportation conditions and temperature records.

### 1.4 Generic, Branded and Innovative Medicines

In addition to prescription status and technology type, medicines can also be classified according to their development background and market positioning.

- **Generic medicines:** Generally developed with reference to an approved reference medicine and required to meet applicable quality and bioequivalence standards.
- **Originator and branded medicines:** Include originator products as well as medicines marketed under a brand name. A branded medicine is not necessarily an originator medicine.
- **Innovative drugs:** Include medicines developed using new active ingredients, mechanisms of action or other innovative technologies. Their classification depends on the relevant regulatory framework.

As China's pharmaceutical research and development and manufacturing capabilities continue to advance, its pharmaceutical market offers international partners a range of products and supply channel options.

## 2. Which Therapeutic Areas Do Chinese Pharmaceutical Suppliers Cover?

![Medical icons representing different organ systems and therapeutic areas](/assets/images/therapeutic-areas-medical-icons.jpeg)

Different therapeutic areas have distinct product characteristics, storage and transportation requirements, and supply arrangements. Oncology, chronic diseases and rare diseases are particularly important areas to consider.

### 2.1 Oncology and Hematology Medicines

Oncology and hematology are important areas within specialty medicine supply. Relevant medicines include:
- **Chemotherapy medicines:** Cytotoxic drugs used to treat different types of cancer.
- **Targeted therapies:** Medicines that act on specific molecular targets or signalling pathways.
- **Immunotherapies:** Including certain immune checkpoint inhibitors and other immunotherapeutic products.
- **Hematology medicines:** Treatments for certain hematological malignancies and other blood disorders.

China's innovative drug development has continued to advance in recent years, with new therapeutic products emerging in oncology and hematology.

### 2.2 Medicines for Chronic Diseases

[Medicines for chronic diseases](https://dengyuemed.github.io/) cover long-term treatment areas such as cardiovascular diseases, diabetes and other metabolic disorders. Product forms include conventional oral formulations, injections and certain biological products.

Supply planning for these medicines generally takes into account product specifications, batch and expiry management, storage requirements and subsequent replenishment arrangements.

### 2.3 Rare Disease and Other Specialty Medicines

[Rare disease medicines](https://dengyuerx.com/product-category/prescription-medicines/rare-diseases/) and other specialty medicines typically target specific conditions or patient populations. Some products involve complex manufacturing processes, special storage and transportation conditions, and more detailed product traceability requirements.

When sourcing these medicines, international buyers need to understand not only product supply information but also supply channels, relevant quality documentation and the import and distribution requirements of the destination market.

Partners with pharmaceutical supply resources and cross-border coordination experience can support product information exchange, supply channel coordination and logistics arrangements.
