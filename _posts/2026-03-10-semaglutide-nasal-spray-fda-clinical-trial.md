## World’s First Semaglutide Nasal Spray Approved by the FDA to Enter Clinical Trials

In recent years, **GLP-1 receptor agonists** have demonstrated strong market potential in the treatment of **diabetes and obesity**. Among them, **semaglutide** has emerged as one of the most successful drugs globally.

Recently, a **semaglutide nasal spray** received approval from the  
<a href="https://www.fda.gov/" target="_blank">U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)</a>  
to enter the clinical research stage, drawing widespread attention across the pharmaceutical industry.

This development suggests that **GLP-1 drugs may no longer be limited to injections or oral tablets** in the future. Nasal spray formulations could represent a **new drug delivery pathway** for this important therapeutic class.

## Semaglutide: A Star Drug in the GLP-1 Field

Semaglutide is a **GLP-1 receptor agonist (GLP-1 RA)** primarily used to treat **type 2 diabetes and obesity**.

By mimicking the natural **GLP-1 hormone**, the drug can:

- Stimulate insulin secretion  
- Suppress glucagon release  
- Slow gastric emptying  
- Increase feelings of fullness  

Currently, semaglutide products developed by  
<a href="https://www.novonordisk.com/" target="_blank">Novo Nordisk</a>  
are widely used worldwide, including:

- **Ozempic** – for diabetes treatment  
- **Wegovy** – for weight management  
- **Rybelsus** – oral semaglutide  

As global **obesity rates continue to rise**, demand for semaglutide is steadily increasing.

## Nasal Spray Formulation: A New Direction for GLP-1 Drugs

Most semaglutide products currently on the market are **injectable or oral formulations**. However, injectable treatments can present **adherence challenges** for some patients.

A **nasal spray formulation** may offer several potential advantages.

### 1. More Convenient Administration

Patients would not need injections or tablets. The medication could simply be delivered through the **nasal cavity**.

### 2. Rapid Absorption

The **nasal mucosa is highly vascularized**, allowing certain drugs to enter the bloodstream rapidly.

### 3. Improved Patient Compliance

For patients requiring **long-term treatment for diabetes or obesity**, non-invasive delivery methods are often easier to accept.

For these reasons, **semaglutide nasal spray** is considered a potential breakthrough **drug-delivery technology** within the GLP-1 therapeutic class.

## What Does FDA Approval for Clinical Trials Mean?

Receiving **FDA approval to enter clinical trials** typically indicates that the drug has completed **preclinical research and safety evaluations**.

The semaglutide nasal spray will now proceed through the standard clinical trial process.

### Phase I Clinical Trials

Evaluate the **drug’s safety and tolerability** in humans.

### Phase II Clinical Trials

Investigate **efficacy and optimal dosage**.

### Phase III Clinical Trials

Large-scale trials designed to confirm **safety and therapeutic effectiveness**.

If these trials are successfully completed, the drug may eventually submit a **New Drug Application (NDA)** to the FDA.

Regulatory approval processes like this are a key step in bringing **innovative therapies into global clinical practice**.
