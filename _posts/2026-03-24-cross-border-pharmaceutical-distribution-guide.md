## How Do Pharmaceuticals Safely “Leave China”?

Pharmaceutical transportation differs significantly from general cargo.

### Transport methods vary by product:

- Innovative drugs / biologics → Air freight  
- APIs → Sea freight (cost-efficient)  
- Conventional drugs → Flexible combination  

### The real core issue is ❄️ temperature control

- Is 2–8°C maintained throughout?  
- Are temperature logs complete?  
- Does packaging meet international standards?  

👉 In pharma logistics, delivery ≠ qualified delivery  

Once temperature control fails, product integrity may be compromised—even if visually intact.

## Customs Declaration — The First Critical Barrier

Many assume logistics providers handle everything.

But in reality, **customs declaration is often the first major risk point**.

### Required documentation:

📄 Basic documents  
- Invoice  
- Packing list  
- Contract  

💊 Pharmaceutical-specific documents  
- Drug registration / filing  
- COA (Certificate of Analysis)  
- Certificate of origin  
- Batch release certificate (if applicable)  

### Key compliance requirements:

- Consistency across all documents  
- Accurate HS code classification  
- Correct product categorization  

❗ Any mismatch can lead to:

- Shipment delays  
- Customs inspection  
- Rejection

## Customs Clearance — The Real Gatekeeper

Even after export approval, the **real challenge often begins at destination**.

Typical clearance steps:

1. Document review  
2. Customs approval  
3. Inspection / sampling  
4. Temperature data verification  

### Common failure points:

- ❌ No local registration  
- ❌ Missing import license  
- ❌ Incomplete temperature records  
- ❌ Document inconsistency  

👉 Most cross-border failures happen here—not during transport.
