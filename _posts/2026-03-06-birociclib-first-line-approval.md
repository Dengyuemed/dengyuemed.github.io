# Birociclib Approved for First-Line Indication  
### China’s First CDK4/6 Inhibitor Covering the Full Spectrum from First- to Late-Line Treatment

Recently, China’s breast cancer treatment landscape has marked another important milestone.

On March 3, the **National Medical Products Administration (NMPA)** announced that **Birociclib tablets (brand name: Xuanyue Ning)**, a CDK4/6 inhibitor independently developed by Xuanzhu Biopharmaceutical, has been approved for a new indication.  

The drug is now authorized **in combination with aromatase inhibitors (AIs)** for the **first-line treatment of hormone receptor–positive / HER2-negative (HR+/HER2−) advanced breast cancer**.

![Birociclib Tablets](/assets/images/birociclib-tablets.png)

With this latest approval, **Birociclib becomes the first and currently the only CDK4/6 inhibitor in China to cover the full treatment spectrum — first-line, second-line, and later-line therapy** for HR+/HER2− advanced breast cancer.

China pharmaceutical wholesaler **DengYueMed** continues to closely follow the development of this innovative therapy.

---

## Mechanism and Drug Design

As a **next-generation CDK4/6 inhibitor**, Birociclib has undergone structural optimization to improve **selectivity for CDK4** while maintaining **certain inhibitory activity against CDK2**.

This mechanism may help:

- enhance **antitumor efficacy**
- maintain **better safety and tolerability**
- support **long-term combination with endocrine therapy**

---

## Phase III Clinical Evidence

The newly approved indication is supported by the **Phase III BRIGHT-3 clinical trial**.

In patients receiving **first-line therapy for HR+/HER2− advanced breast cancer**, Birociclib combined with **letrozole or anastrozole** showed significant advantages compared with placebo plus endocrine therapy:

- **47% reduction** in the risk of disease progression or death
- **Objective response rate (ORR) of 63.5%**, compared with 42.5% in the control group
- **64% reduction in progression risk** in patients with liver metastases

These results reinforce Birociclib’s strong competitiveness in the **first-line treatment setting**.

## Market Landscape of CDK4/6 Inhibitors

Breast cancer remains one of the most prevalent cancers worldwide, and the therapeutic market continues to expand rapidly.

Industry forecasts suggest that by **2030 the global breast cancer treatment market may approach USD 70 billion**.

Currently, several CDK4/6 inhibitors are already approved in China, including:

- Dalpiciclib (Hengrui Pharma)
- Abemaciclib (Eli Lilly)
- Ribociclib (Novartis)
- Palbociclib (Pfizer)

Against this competitive background, **Birociclib’s ability to cover the entire treatment line provides a differentiated clinical positioning.**

## NRDL Inclusion Improves Patient Access

Another key milestone occurred in **December 2025**, when Birociclib was included in China’s **National Reimbursement Drug List (NRDL)**.

This inclusion significantly improves:

- patient affordability
- clinical accessibility
- market penetration potential
