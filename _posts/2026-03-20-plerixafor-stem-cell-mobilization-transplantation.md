## I. The Core Clinical Challenge: Mobilization Failure

Traditional mobilization strategies rely primarily on granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (G-CSF).

However, clinical data show:

- **10–30% of patients respond poorly**
- Insufficient CD34+ cell counts (<2×10⁶/kg)
- Increased risk of transplant delay or failure

These limitations create a major bottleneck in transplantation accessibility.

## II. Mechanism of Action: Breaking the CXCR4–SDF-1 Axis

Plerixafor works by:

- Blocking CXCR4 receptors  
- Disrupting CXCR4–SDF-1α binding  
- Releasing stem cells from bone marrow into peripheral blood  

When combined with G-CSF:

- Mobilization efficiency significantly increases  
- Even “poor mobilizers” can achieve target collection  

This **mechanism-driven synergy** represents a major advancement in transplantation medicine.
