In recent years, with the continuous updates of authoritative clinical guidelines from the Chinese Society of Clinical Oncology (CSCO), the National Comprehensive Cancer Network (NCCN), and the European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO), [oncology treatment](https://dengyuemed.com/oncology-diseases/) has been rapidly evolving from the era of conventional chemotherapy toward precision medicine and immunotherapy.

Between 2025 and 2026, multiple landmark Phase III clinical trials were incorporated into the latest treatment guidelines, including antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs), bispecific antibodies, immune checkpoint inhibitors (PD-1/PD-L1), and novel targeted therapies.

Notably, several Chinese-origin innovative drugs achieved historic breakthroughs by being incorporated into frontline treatment recommendations across multiple tumor types, marking China's transition from a follower to a global innovator—and in some areas, a leader—in oncology drug development.

This article reviews the most significant frontline treatment updates and emerging trends based on the latest 2026 CSCO guidelines, as well as updates from ASCO, ESMO, and NCCN.

---

## Urothelial Carcinoma: DV + Toripalimab Becomes a Category I Recommendation

The 2026 edition of the CSCO Guidelines for Urothelial Carcinoma introduced one of its most important updates: the combination of **Disitamab Vedotin (DV, RC48)** and **Toripalimab** was added as a **Category I recommendation (Level 1A evidence)** for first-line treatment of:

- Metastatic bladder urothelial carcinoma
- Upper tract urothelial carcinoma (UTUC)

The recommendation applies to both:

- Cisplatin-eligible patients
- Cisplatin-ineligible patients

### RC48-C016 Trial Highlights

Compared with platinum-based chemotherapy, DV + Toripalimab demonstrated:

- Nearly doubled objective response rate (ORR)
- Significant improvement in progression-free survival (PFS)
- Meaningful overall survival (OS) benefit

Results from the international **RC48-G001** study also confirmed efficacy in Western populations, supporting broader global adoption.

## Lung Cancer: Chinese Innovation Challenges Global Standards

[Lung cancer](https://dengyuemed.com/product/lung-cancer/) remains the leading cause of cancer-related mortality worldwide.

### EGFR-Mutated NSCLC

Recommended first-line regimens continue to include:

- [Osimertinib](https://dengyuemed.com/product/tarisso-osimertinib-nsclc/)
- Osimertinib plus chemotherapy
- Osimertinib plus VEGF inhibition

### ALK-Rearranged NSCLC

Preferred options include:

- Alectinib
- Lorlatinib

### High PD-L1 Expression

Immunotherapy alone remains strongly recommended:

- [Pembrolizumab](https://dengyuemed.com/product/keytruda-pembrolizumab-nsclc/)
- [Tislelizumab](https://dengyuemed.com/product/tevimbra-tislelizumab-jsgr-nsclc/)
- [Sintilimab](https://dengyuemed.com/product/daboshu-sintilimab-injection-lung-cancer/)

Among the most closely watched innovations is **Ivonescimab**, China's PD-1/VEGF bispecific antibody, which has shown highly promising Phase III clinical results.

## Breast Cancer Enters the ADC Era

Recent guideline updates emphasize earlier use of antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs).

### Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC)

Sacituzumab Govitecan has gained stronger recommendations, particularly for:

- PD-L1-positive disease
- Patients previously treated with taxanes
- High-risk recurrence populations

### HER2-Low Breast Cancer

The recognition of HER2-low disease has expanded treatment opportunities.

Recommended ADCs include:

- Trastuzumab Deruxtecan (T-DXd)
- Next-generation HER2 ADCs

---

## Gastric Cancer: Biomarker-Driven Precision Therapy

Treatment decisions are increasingly guided by molecular biomarkers rather than histology alone.

Important biomarkers include:

- HER2
- PD-L1 CPS
- Claudin 18.2
- FGFR2b
- MSI-H / dMMR

### Claudin 18.2

CLDN18.2-targeted therapies continue to gain momentum globally and are expected to become an important component of gastric cancer treatment.

### Chinese PD-1 Inhibitors

First-line recommendations increasingly include:

- Sintilimab
- Tislelizumab
- Cadonilimab
- Toripalimab

# Four Major Trends in the 2026 Oncology Guidelines

## 1. ADCs Move Earlier

ADCs are rapidly moving into:

- First-line treatment
- Neoadjuvant therapy
- Perioperative therapy

---

## 2. Bispecific Antibodies Continue Rapid Growth

Bispecific antibodies are expected to reshape treatment across:

- Lung cancer
- Gastric cancer
- [Colorectal cancer](https://dengyuemed.com/product/colorectal-cancer/)
- [Breast cancer](https://dengyuemed.com/product/breast-cancer/)

---

## 3. Precision Diagnostics Become Essential

Clinical decision-making increasingly relies on:

- Next-generation sequencing (NGS)
- Immunohistochemistry (IHC)
- Liquid biopsy
- Circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA)

Precision diagnosis has become the gateway to precision treatment.

---

## 4. Chinese Innovative Drugs Go Global

During 2025–2026:

- ADC licensing reached record highs
- Bispecific antibody partnerships expanded
- CAR-T therapies accelerated internationally
- Multiple Chinese drugs entered FDA and EMA review pathways

China's role in global oncology innovation continues to grow.

# Clinical Implications

Important recommendations include:

- Earlier molecular profiling
- Multidisciplinary team (MDT) management
- Personalized treatment strategies
- Proactive management of ADC and immune-related toxicities
- Continuous MRD and resistance monitoring

Treatment decisions are becoming increasingly biomarker-driven rather than histology-driven.
