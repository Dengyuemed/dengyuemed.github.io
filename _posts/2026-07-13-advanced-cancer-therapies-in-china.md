# CAR-T Cell Therapy: A Major Breakthrough for Hematologic Malignancies

![CAR-T Cell Therapy](/assets/images/car-t-cell-therapy.png)

## What Is CAR-T?

CAR-T (Chimeric Antigen Receptor T-cell Therapy) is one of the most important breakthroughs in modern cellular immunotherapy.

Unlike conventional chemotherapy or radiation therapy, CAR-T treatment uses the patient's own immune cells to recognize and destroy cancer cells.

The treatment process generally includes several highly specialized steps:

1. Collecting T cells from the patient's blood
2. Genetically modifying the cells in a GMP-certified laboratory
3. Expanding the engineered cells
4. Performing rigorous quality-control testing
5. Transporting the cells under validated cold-chain conditions
6. Reinfusing the modified cells back into the patient

After infusion, the engineered CAR-T cells can specifically recognize cancer-associated antigens and launch targeted immune attacks against malignant cells.

Because every product is manufactured individually for each patient, CAR-T therapy requires sophisticated laboratory infrastructure, experienced clinical teams, and comprehensive regulatory oversight.

---

## Which Diseases Currently Benefit the Most?

CAR-T therapy has demonstrated the strongest clinical evidence in hematologic malignancies, including:

- [Relapsed or refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL)](https://dengyuemed.com/product/lymphoma/)
- Acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL)
- Multiple myeloma
- Mantle cell lymphoma
- Follicular lymphoma
- Other selected B-cell malignancies

Several [CAR-T products](https://dengyuemed.com/blog/approved-cell-and-gene-therapy-drugs-in-china/) have already received regulatory approval in China, while hundreds of additional clinical trials continue exploring new targets and treatment strategies.

China has become one of the world's leading centers for CAR-T clinical research, with continuous expansion into next-generation technologies.

---

## Which International Patients May Be Suitable?

CAR-T therapy may be considered for international patients who:

- Have relapsed after multiple previous treatments
- Have refractory hematologic malignancies
- Cannot tolerate additional chemotherapy
- Are seeking innovative immunotherapy options
- Face prolonged waiting periods in their home countries

Compared with some countries, eligible international patients may benefit from:

- Faster treatment scheduling
- Broader clinical trial availability
- Greater physician experience with large patient volumes
- More affordable treatment costs in selected cases

However, CAR-T therapy is **not** appropriate for every patient.

For many common solid tumors—including lung cancer, pancreatic cancer, colorectal cancer, gastric cancer, and liver cancer—CAR-T remains largely under clinical investigation, although promising research continues worldwide.

Every patient should undergo comprehensive medical evaluation before determining whether CAR-T therapy is an appropriate treatment option.

# Immunotherapy: Reactivating the Body's Anti-Cancer Immune Response

![Cancer Immunotherapy](/assets/images/immunotherapy-treatment.png)

## What Is Immunotherapy?

Immunotherapy is one of the most important advances in modern oncology. Instead of attacking cancer cells directly, immunotherapy helps the body's own immune system recognize and eliminate malignant cells more effectively.

Many cancer cells evade immune surveillance by activating immune checkpoint pathways. Checkpoint inhibitors block these pathways, allowing immune cells to recover their ability to identify and destroy tumor cells.

The most widely used immune checkpoint inhibitors include:

- [PD-1 inhibitors](https://dengyuemed.com/blog/approved-immune-checkpoint-inhibitors-in-china/)
- [PD-L1 inhibitors](https://dengyuemed.com/blog/approved-immune-checkpoint-inhibitors-in-china/)
- [CTLA-4 inhibitors](https://dengyuemed.com/blog/approved-immune-checkpoint-inhibitors-in-china/)

Over the past several years, China has approved numerous immunotherapy medicines, including both internationally developed products and domestically developed innovative therapies.

These medicines are now routinely incorporated into treatment strategies for many advanced cancers.

---

## Which Cancers May Benefit?

Immunotherapy has demonstrated clinical benefit across a wide range of malignancies, including:

- [Non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC)](https://dengyuemed.com/product/lung-cancer/)
- [Hepatocellular carcinoma (liver cancer)](https://dengyuemed.com/product/liver-cancer/)
- [Melanoma](https://dengyuemed.com/product/melanoma/)
- Nasopharyngeal carcinoma
- Esophageal cancer
- [Gastric cancer](https://dengyuemed.com/product/stomach-cancer/)
- Renal cell carcinoma
- Cervical cancer
- Urothelial carcinoma
- Hodgkin lymphoma

For many tumor types, immunotherapy is now combined with chemotherapy, targeted therapy, anti-angiogenic therapy, or radiation therapy to improve treatment outcomes.

China has also accumulated extensive clinical experience in treating Asian patient populations, particularly for liver cancer, nasopharyngeal carcinoma, and gastrointestinal malignancies.

---

## Which International Patients May Be Suitable?

Immunotherapy may be considered for patients who:

- Have advanced or metastatic cancer
- Are not candidates for surgery
- Have experienced disease progression after chemotherapy
- Wish to explore longer-term disease control strategies
- May benefit from combination treatment approaches

However, immunotherapy is **not suitable for every patient**.

Before treatment begins, physicians typically evaluate several important biomarkers, including:

- PD-L1 expression level
- MSI (Microsatellite Instability)
- TMB (Tumor Mutational Burden)
- Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS)
- Driver gene mutations
- Overall tumor type and stage

Some patients achieve long-lasting responses, while others may derive limited benefit. Comprehensive biomarker testing therefore plays an essential role in treatment planning.

# Proton and Heavy Ion Therapy: High-Precision Radiation Treatment

![Proton and Heavy Ion Therapy](/assets/images/proton-heavy-ion-center.png)

## What Is Proton or Heavy Ion Therapy?

Proton therapy and heavy ion therapy represent some of the world's most advanced forms of radiation oncology.

Unlike conventional X-ray radiotherapy, particle therapy delivers radiation with much greater precision through a physical property known as the **Bragg Peak**.

This allows radiation energy to be concentrated directly within the tumor while minimizing unnecessary exposure to surrounding healthy tissues.

Potential advantages include:

- Higher radiation precision
- Better protection of nearby healthy organs
- Lower radiation exposure outside the tumor
- Reduced long-term treatment-related toxicity
- Improved preservation of organ function

For tumors located near critical anatomical structures, these advantages can be particularly valuable.

---

## Which Cancers May Benefit?

Proton or heavy ion therapy may be considered for selected patients with:

- Nasopharyngeal carcinoma
- Brain tumors
- Skull base tumors
- Chordoma
- Chondrosarcoma
- Pediatric cancers
- [Prostate cancer](https://dengyuemed.com/product/prostate-cancer/)
- Liver cancer
- Pancreatic cancer
- Selected recurrent tumors

These therapies are especially useful when tumors are located close to sensitive structures such as:

- Brain stem
- Spinal cord
- Eyes
- Optic nerves
- Major blood vessels
- Heart
- Liver

By reducing unnecessary radiation to surrounding tissues, particle therapy may decrease certain treatment-related complications.

---

Several leading cancer centers now operate world-class proton and heavy ion treatment facilities equipped with highly sophisticated imaging, treatment planning, and quality assurance systems.

Compared with several Western countries, treatment costs in China may also be substantially lower while maintaining modern treatment standards.

# NanoKnife (Irreversible Electroporation): A New Option for Difficult-to-Treat Tumors

![NanoKnife Therapy](/assets/images/nanoknife-treatment.png)

## What Is NanoKnife?

NanoKnife, also known as **Irreversible Electroporation (IRE)**, is an innovative minimally invasive tumor ablation technology.

Unlike thermal ablation techniques that destroy tumors using heat or freezing temperatures, NanoKnife delivers precisely controlled, high-voltage electrical pulses through specially positioned electrodes.

These electrical pulses permanently disrupt cancer cell membranes, leading to programmed cell death while largely preserving important surrounding structures.

Because no extreme heat is generated, NanoKnife can often avoid damaging:

- Major blood vessels
- Bile ducts
- Nerves
- Connective tissue

This unique characteristic makes NanoKnife particularly valuable for tumors located in anatomically complex regions.

---

## Which Cancers Commonly Use NanoKnife?

NanoKnife has been increasingly applied in carefully selected patients with:

- Pancreatic cancer
- Liver cancer
- Prostate cancer
- Kidney tumors
- Selected lung cancers
- Pelvic tumors

Its greatest clinical value is often seen in patients with **locally advanced tumors** that cannot be safely removed through conventional surgery.

Rather than replacing surgery, NanoKnife may become part of a multidisciplinary treatment strategy alongside chemotherapy, targeted therapy, immunotherapy, or radiation therapy.

---

## Which International Patients May Benefit?

NanoKnife may be appropriate for patients who:

- Have unresectable locally advanced tumors
- Are not suitable surgical candidates
- Require minimally invasive treatment
- Hope to preserve important blood vessels or nerves
- Have experienced limited benefit from standard therapies

Treatment eligibility depends on multiple clinical factors, including tumor size, location, imaging findings, and overall health status.

Comprehensive evaluation by an experienced multidisciplinary oncology team remains essential before proceeding with NanoKnife treatment.

# Cryoablation: Minimally Invasive Precision Tumor Destruction

![Cryoablation Therapy](/assets/images/cryoablation-treatment.png)

## What Is Cryoablation?

Cryoablation is a minimally invasive interventional oncology technique that destroys tumor tissue by exposing it to extremely low temperatures.

During the procedure, physicians insert specially designed cryoprobes into the tumor under imaging guidance such as CT or ultrasound. Argon gas rapidly cools the probe tip, creating an "ice ball" that freezes and destroys cancer cells while preserving as much surrounding healthy tissue as possible.

Compared with conventional surgery, cryoablation offers several potential advantages:

- Smaller incisions
- Reduced blood loss
- Faster recovery
- Shorter hospital stays
- Repeatable treatment if necessary
- Lower procedural trauma for selected patients

Because the procedure is image-guided, physicians can precisely monitor the treatment area throughout the ablation process.

---

## Which Cancers May Benefit?

Cryoablation has been used in selected patients with:

- Lung cancer
- Liver cancer
- Kidney cancer
- Bone metastases
- Soft tissue tumors
- Selected breast tumors

It may also be used for palliative treatment in patients with painful bone metastases or tumors causing significant symptoms.

Although cryoablation is not appropriate for every patient, it can provide meaningful local tumor control in carefully selected cases.

---

## Which International Patients May Benefit?

Cryoablation may be considered for:

- Elderly patients unable to tolerate major surgery
- Patients with localized tumors
- Individuals requiring minimally invasive treatment
- Patients seeking symptom relief
- Patients wishing to preserve organ function whenever possible

Treatment suitability depends on tumor size, anatomical location, previous treatments, and overall health condition.

# Precision Targeted Therapy: Personalized Treatment Based on Genetic Testing

![Precision Targeted Therapy](/assets/images/precision-targeted-therapy.png)

## Entering the Era of Precision Medicine

Cancer treatment has increasingly shifted from traditional "one-size-fits-all" therapy toward personalized precision medicine.

Instead of selecting treatment solely according to where the cancer originated, physicians now analyze the tumor's genetic profile to identify actionable mutations that can be matched with targeted medicines.

Modern precision oncology commonly includes:

- Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS)
- Molecular profiling
- Biomarker testing
- Companion diagnostic testing
- Liquid biopsy in selected situations

This approach allows doctors to design more individualized treatment plans for eligible patients.

---

## Common Molecular Targets

China has become one of the world's leading centers for targeted oncology drug development.

Common therapeutic targets include:

- EGFR
- ALK
- ROS1
- HER2
- KRAS
- RET
- MET
- BRAF
- NTRK

Numerous targeted medicines developed in China have also gained international recognition through global clinical studies.

---

## Which Patients May Benefit?

Precision targeted therapy may be especially appropriate for patients with:

- Non-small cell lung cancer
- Breast cancer
- Colorectal cancer
- Gastric cancer
- Ovarian cancer
- Thyroid cancer
- Cholangiocarcinoma
- Other tumors carrying actionable genetic alterations

Compared with conventional chemotherapy, targeted therapies often produce fewer systemic side effects because they are designed to attack specific molecular abnormalities.

However, targeted therapy is effective only when the relevant mutation or biomarker is present.

For this reason, comprehensive molecular testing is an essential first step before treatment decisions are made.
