## Introduction

As precision medicine becomes the global standard in cancer treatment and CAR-T cell therapy continues to break through the limitations of advanced cancer care, China’s JCI-accredited cancer hospitals are emerging as preferred destinations for international patients seeking cross-border medical treatment.

With internationally aligned medical expertise and cost-effective healthcare services, these hospitals are gaining growing recognition worldwide.

JCI (Joint Commission International) is regarded as the global gold standard for healthcare quality and patient safety.

A JCI accreditation indicates that a hospital has achieved internationally recognized standards in diagnosis, treatment, safety management, and patient-centered care.

In recent years, the number of international patients choosing China’s JCI cancer hospitals has grown steadily, with patients arriving from:

- Southeast Asia
- Central Asia
- Europe
- North America

Many patients choose China because Chinese JCI hospitals can provide precision oncology and innovative therapies comparable to leading Western medical centers while also addressing concerns such as:

- High treatment costs
- Long waiting times
- Limited treatment accessibility

China is gradually reshaping the perception that high-quality cancer care can only be found in Europe or the United States.

One of the core strengths of China’s JCI cancer hospitals is precision medicine.

These hospitals have introduced internationally advanced diagnostic technologies, including:

- Genomic sequencing

China’s JCI cancer hospitals have developed mature precision treatment systems for common cancers such as:

## CAR-T Therapy: New Hope for Advanced Cancer Patients

If precision medicine forms the foundation of China’s oncology advancement, CAR-T cell therapy represents one of its most important breakthroughs.

CAR-T therapy is one of the world’s most advanced cancer treatments and is primarily used for:

- Relapsed lymphoma
- Refractory leukemia
- Multiple myeloma
- Certain hematologic malignancies

In some cases, objective response rates exceed 60%, offering new hope to patients with advanced cancers.

China’s JCI cancer hospitals have rapidly expanded CAR-T clinical applications, keeping pace with leading Western medical centers and achieving breakthroughs in several areas.

China now accounts for a significant proportion of global cell therapy clinical trials and has become one of the most active regions in CAR-T innovation.

Many Chinese JCI hospitals routinely provide CAR-T therapy for diseases such as:

- Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma (DLBCL)
- Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia (ALL)
- Multiple Myeloma (MM)

Importantly, treatment costs are often substantially lower than those in Europe or the United States, significantly reducing the financial burden on international patients.

In addition to CAR-T therapy, Guangzhou RoyalLee Cancer Hospital is actively developing innovative treatment programs through strategic cooperation with Shenzhen Cell Valley, aiming to establish international cell therapy channels and expand future CAR-T services.

The hospital also provides advanced minimally invasive treatments such as:

- Y-90 microsphere therapy
- Proton therapy
- NanoKnife treatment
- Cryoablation therapies

These technologies help create a diversified cancer treatment system tailored to different patient needs.
