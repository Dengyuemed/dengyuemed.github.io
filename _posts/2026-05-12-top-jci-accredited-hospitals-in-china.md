## Introduction

For international patients, choosing a hospital with global medical standards, comprehensive multilingual services, and extensive experience in international patient management is extremely important.

- Cancer treatment

As China’s healthcare system continues to internationalize, more overseas patients are considering China as a destination for advanced medical treatment.

---

# What Is JCI Accreditation and Why Is It Important for International Patients?

JCI (Joint Commission International) accreditation is an internationally recognized healthcare quality and patient safety certification system.

It is widely regarded as one of the most important benchmarks for evaluating international medical services.

For overseas patients, a JCI-accredited hospital usually indicates:

- Standardized international medical procedures
- Advanced patient safety management systems
- Multilingual medical service capabilities
- International insurance direct billing services
- Extensive experience serving overseas patients
- Mature cross-border medical coordination systems

As a result, JCI-accredited hospitals are often preferred destinations for international patients seeking treatment in China.

# Why Are More International Patients Choosing China for Medical Treatment?

In the past, many overseas patients traveled to countries such as the United States, Japan, or Singapore for advanced medical treatment.

However, China’s cross-border healthcare industry has expanded rapidly in recent years.

---

## 1. Significant Improvements in Medical Technology

China has made substantial progress in:

- Minimally invasive oncology treatments
- AI-assisted diagnosis
- Cell therapy
- Precision medicine

Some treatment technologies are now reaching internationally advanced levels.

---

## 2. More Cost-Effective Treatment Options

Compared with Western countries, many advanced medical treatments in China are more affordable, especially for patients requiring long-term care.

This cost advantage is attracting increasing numbers of international patients.

---

## 3. Continuous Improvement in International Healthcare Services

An increasing number of hospitals in China have established:

- International departments
- VIP wards
- Multilingual medical systems
- International insurance cooperation services

These developments make the treatment process more convenient for overseas patients.

---

## 4. Faster Access to Innovative Drugs and Therapies

Policy platforms such as the Hainan Boao Lecheng International Medical Tourism Pilot Zone have accelerated access to internationally innovative drugs and medical devices.

This provides patients with broader treatment options and faster access to advanced therapies.
