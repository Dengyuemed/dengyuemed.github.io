## What Is Tiotropium Bromide and Olodaterol Hydrochloride Inhalation Spray?

![Spiolto Respimat](/assets/images/spiolto-respimat-product.png)

**Tiotropium Bromide and Olodaterol Hydrochloride Inhalation Spray ([Spiolto Respimat®](https://dengyuemed.com/product/spiolto-respimat-olodaterol-copd/))** is a fixed-dose **LAMA/LABA combination inhalation therapy** indicated for the long-term maintenance treatment of **chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD)**, including chronic bronchitis and emphysema.

It helps:

- Relieve persistent shortness of breath
- Improve lung function
- Reduce COPD exacerbations
- Improve quality of life

The product combines two long-acting bronchodilators.

### 1. Tiotropium

Tiotropium is a **long-acting muscarinic antagonist (LAMA)**.

It blocks M3 muscarinic receptors in airway smooth muscle, producing sustained bronchodilation and improving airflow limitation.

### 2. Olodaterol

Olodaterol is a **long-acting β₂-adrenergic receptor agonist (LABA)**.

It relaxes airway smooth muscle by activating β₂ receptors, providing additional bronchodilation.

Together, these complementary mechanisms provide **24-hour bronchodilation**, allowing effective **once-daily maintenance therapy**.

## What Does China's First Generic Approval Mean?

The approval of China's first generic [Tiotropium Bromide and Olodaterol Hydrochloride](https://dengyuemed.com/product/spiolto-respimat-olodaterol-copd/) Inhalation Spray represents an important milestone for both patients and the domestic pharmaceutical industry.

According to publicly available information:

- The originator product was developed by **Boehringer Ingelheim**
- It was first approved in China in **2018**
- Soft mist inhalers are among the most technically challenging respiratory drug products

For many years, no domestic manufacturer successfully developed a generic version.

The simultaneous approval of products from:

- Nanchang Gusen Biopharmaceutical Co., Ltd.
- Aitme (Suzhou) Medical Technology Co., Ltd.

This achievement:

- Expands treatment options for COPD patients
- Promotes localization of complex inhalation formulations
- Strengthens China's inhalation drug manufacturing capabilities
- Provides healthcare professionals with additional therapeutic choices
- May improve accessibility and affordability

## Why Is This Considered a High-End Complex Formulation?

Compared with conventional tablets or injections, inhalation products require both sophisticated pharmaceutical formulations and highly precise drug-device combination systems.

The approved product uses **Soft Mist Inhaler (SMI)** technology.

Advantages include:

- No propellant required
- Fine mist generation through mechanical energy
- Lower spray velocity for improved lung deposition
- Easier inhalation for elderly patients
- Reduced coordination requirements
- Better treatment adherence

Because soft mist inhalers integrate:

- Pharmaceutical formulation
- Precision engineering
- Aerosol science
- Device technology

they are widely regarded as one of the most technically demanding dosage forms in respiratory medicine.

## Why Do COPD Patients Need Long-Term Maintenance Therapy?

COPD is a progressive respiratory disease characterized by persistent airflow limitation.

It mainly includes:

- Chronic bronchitis
- Emphysema

Common symptoms include:

- Chronic cough
- Sputum production
- Shortness of breath
- Wheezing
- Chest tightness
- Progressive decline in lung function

Although COPD cannot currently be cured, long-term maintenance therapy can:

- Relieve symptoms
- Improve pulmonary function
- Reduce exacerbations
- Lower hospitalization rates
- Improve quality of life
