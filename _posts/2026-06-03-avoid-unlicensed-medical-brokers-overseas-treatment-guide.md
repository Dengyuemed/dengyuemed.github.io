In recent years, with the increasing accessibility of global healthcare resources and the growing demand for cross-border medical services, more and more patients have turned their attention to overseas healthcare options. From precision cancer therapies and CAR-T cell treatment to innovative drugs for rare diseases and international expert consultations, overseas medical care has brought new hope to many patients.

However, behind this rapidly expanding market, a growing number of unlicensed medical brokers have emerged under the banners of "overseas healthcare," "international referrals," "innovative therapies," and "specialized drug sourcing." Taking advantage of patients' urgency to seek treatment and the information gap that often exists, these brokers use misleading advertisements, exaggerated claims, and even fabricated partnerships to generate substantial profits.

The consequences can extend far beyond financial losses—they may delay critical treatment opportunities and cause irreversible harm to patients.

## What Are Unlicensed Medical Brokers?

Unlicensed medical brokers are organizations or individuals that provide services such as overseas medical consultations, international referrals, access to new medications, or cell therapy recommendations without possessing the necessary qualifications or professional healthcare credentials.

These entities often lack medical expertise and legitimate international healthcare partnerships. Rather than helping patients access suitable medical resources, their primary objective is often to profit from information asymmetry by charging excessive service fees.

In some cases, these brokers have been found to forge partnership documents, impersonate well-known medical experts, or fabricate successful treatment stories in order to gain patients' trust.

---

## The Five Most Common Scams Used by Medical Brokers

### 1. Fabricating Access to International Medical Experts

Patients frequently encounter promotional claims such as:

- Direct treatment by world-renowned oncology experts
- Fast-track access to leading U.S. hospitals
- Exclusive partnerships with overseas medical institutions
- Consultations with global expert panels

However, after paying substantial fees, patients often discover that the actual physicians involved bear little resemblance to those advertised, or that the claimed partnerships never existed in the first place.

Legitimate healthcare providers do not rely on exaggerated marketing. Instead, they provide transparent and verifiable information about hospitals and physicians.

### 2. Exaggerating Treatment Outcomes

Warning signs include claims such as:

- More than 90% cure rate
- Complete recovery without chemotherapy
- Guaranteed effectiveness with the latest technology
- One treatment provides lifelong benefits

Whenever patients encounter such statements, they should exercise extreme caution.

Medicine is a rigorous science. Even the most advanced treatments available today cannot guarantee identical outcomes for every patient. Reputable hospitals and physicians never make absolute promises regarding treatment success.

Any organization that claims to "guarantee a cure" or "ensure effectiveness" should be considered highly suspicious.

### 3. Exploiting Popular Therapies Such as CAR-T and Stem Cell Treatments

Innovative therapies such as CAR-T cell therapy, stem cell therapy, and NK cell therapy have attracted significant attention worldwide.

Some brokers exploit patients' limited understanding of these emerging technologies by promoting offers such as:

- Low-cost CAR-T programs
- Customized private CAR-T treatments
- Direct access to overseas laboratory therapies
- Treatment without regulatory approval

In reality, legitimate cell therapies require strict manufacturing standards, quality control procedures, and regulatory oversight. Products of unknown origin or produced outside approved facilities may pose serious safety risks.

### 4. Charging Fees Under the Pretense of Clinical Trial Enrollment

Clinical trials can provide patients with valuable access to innovative therapies. Unfortunately, they are also frequently exploited by fraudulent intermediaries.

Some organizations falsely claim:

- Guaranteed enrollment in international clinical trials for a fee
- Priority access after paying a service charge
- Eligibility through the purchase of specific medications

In reality, clinical trials operate under strict medical and scientific criteria. Enrollment decisions are made solely by the research team based on a patient's medical condition and eligibility requirements. Participation cannot be purchased.

### 5. Supplying Medicines from Unverified Sources

For patients urgently seeking innovative therapies, medication safety is especially important.

Some brokers offer:

- Overseas miracle drugs
- The latest anticancer medications
- Newly developed drugs not yet available locally
- Rare disease treatments in limited supply

These products may originate from unverified sources, be transported improperly, stored under unsuitable conditions, or even be counterfeit.

As a result, patients may not only fail to receive the expected therapeutic benefits but may also face serious health risks.

## How to Identify a Legitimate Medical Service Provider

When selecting an overseas medical service provider, patients should carefully evaluate the following factors.

### Verify Business Credentials

A reputable medical service provider should have:

- Legal business registration
- A physical office location
- Publicly available contact information

### Confirm Hospital and Physician Credentials

Patients should independently verify through official hospital websites:

- Whether the hospital genuinely exists
- Whether the physician is currently practicing
- Whether the physician possesses appropriate specialist qualifications

### Request a Formal Service Agreement

Legitimate service providers clearly define:

- Scope of services
- Fee structure
- Refund policies
- Responsibilities of all parties

Organizations that rely solely on messaging apps and refuse to provide written agreements should be approached with caution.

### Be Wary of Extremely Low Prices and Absolute Claims

Healthcare services involve significant expertise and complexity.

If a treatment is priced dramatically below market rates or repeatedly advertised using phrases such as "guaranteed success" or "100% effective," further verification is essential.

---

## Key Questions to Ask Before Pursuing Overseas Medical Treatment

Before making a decision, patients should consider the following questions:

- Is the hospital information authentic and verifiable?
- Can the physician's credentials be independently confirmed?
- Has the treatment been approved by the relevant regulatory authority?
- Are all costs clearly disclosed?
- Has a formal service agreement been provided?
- Is the medication source transparent and traceable?

Only when these questions have been satisfactorily answered should patients proceed with an overseas treatment plan.
