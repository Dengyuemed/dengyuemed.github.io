For international visitors traveling, working, or staying in China temporarily, experiencing a cold, headache, allergy, or stomach discomfort at night can be stressful. Is there a pharmacy still open nearby? How can you find the right medicine if you are unfamiliar with Chinese?

Some pharmacies in China operate 24 hours a day, and certain online pharmacy platforms offer medicine delivery services. However, availability depends on the city, pharmacy opening hours, medicine stock, and delivery coverage. Understanding nighttime pharmacy services in China can help tourists manage common health needs more confidently during their trip.

## Are There 24-Hour Pharmacies in China?

Yes. Some large pharmacy chains and selected branches in China operate 24 hours a day, allowing customers to purchase medicines at night. However, not all pharmacies are open around the clock, and opening hours may vary between branches of the same chain.

Tourists can start by checking large pharmacy chains, community pharmacies, and pharmacies near hospitals. Before setting out, use a map application, the pharmacy's official channels, or an online pharmacy platform to confirm opening hours and check whether the required medicine is in stock.

## How Can You Quickly Find a Nearby Pharmacy at Night?

If you need medicine at night, consider the following options:

1. **Use a map application.** Search for "24-hour pharmacy," "pharmacy open at night," or "nearby pharmacy" to find locations and opening hours. Map information may not always be up to date, so it is best to confirm before leaving.
2. **Check online pharmacy platforms.** Some platforms display nearby pharmacies, medicine information, and delivery options. Whether you can place an order and receive delivery at night depends on the actual services available.
3. **Ask hotel staff for assistance.** If you are unfamiliar with Chinese, ask the hotel reception to help you locate a nearby pharmacy that is open. You can use the following English phrase: "Is there a 24-hour pharmacy nearby?"

## What Medicines and Healthcare Products Can You Find at Night?

Pharmacies in China generally offer a variety of medicines and everyday healthcare products. However, the actual selection depends on each pharmacy's business scope and stock.

| Common need | Medicines or products that may be available |
|---|---|
| Cold symptoms and sore throat | Certain cold medicines and cough medicines |
| Fever or pain | Certain fever-reducing and pain-relieving medicines |
| Allergy symptoms | Certain antihistamines |
| Minor cuts and scrapes | Adhesive bandages and sterile dressings |
| Routine health monitoring | Thermometers and other health-monitoring products |

When purchasing medicine, check the active ingredients, intended users, dosage instructions, and contraindications based on your individual circumstances. If you are unsure which product to choose, consult a pharmacist and avoid taking multiple medicines containing the same active ingredient.

Please note that prescription medicines must be dispensed in accordance with applicable prescription and review requirements. These requirements still apply when purchasing medicine at night.

## What Should International Tourists Know About Buying Medicine at Night?

**First, keep a record of your medicine information.** If you regularly take a particular medicine, record its generic name, active ingredients, and strength. Keep the original packaging and package insert whenever possible. Brand names can differ between countries, so medicines should not be assumed to be equivalent based on their names alone.

**Second, check the packaging and expiration date.** After receiving your medicine, confirm the product name and strength, check that the packaging is intact, and verify the expiration date. Store the medicine according to the instructions provided.

**Third, pay attention to language barriers.** If you cannot understand the Chinese package insert, ask the pharmacist to explain the essential information or use a translation tool to help you understand it. Children, pregnant women, older adults, and people with chronic conditions should pay particular attention to medicine precautions and contraindications.

## Can You Buy Medicine Online If Pharmacies Are Closed?

In some parts of China, compliant online pharmacy platforms offer medicine searches, ordering, and delivery services. Tourists can search for a medicine by name, check its strength and intended use, and then review whether nearby pharmacies have it in stock, are accepting orders, and can deliver within the required timeframe.

According to the National Medical Products Administration (NMPA), China's Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Online Drug Sales establish requirements concerning business qualifications, medicine quality management, and prescription review for online medicine sales.

Therefore, when purchasing medicine online, do not focus solely on price and delivery speed. Check the qualifications of the platform and the business operating the pharmacy. Some medicines require special storage conditions, so appropriate transportation and storage should also be considered.

If your symptoms worsen or you are unsure whether your condition is serious, do not simply wait for a medicine delivery. Seek medical assistance promptly.

## When Should You Seek Medical Care Instead of Visiting a Pharmacy?

![Hospital emergency entrance at night with an ambulance and medical staff](/assets/images/hospital-emergency-entrance-night.jpg)

Pharmacies can provide certain common over-the-counter medicines and healthcare products, but they cannot replace medical diagnosis and treatment.

Seek emergency medical assistance if you experience difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness, severe chest pain, significant bleeding, or a severe allergic reaction.

In mainland China, the medical emergency number is 120. Do not delay emergency treatment while searching for a pharmacy or waiting for a medicine delivery.

Persistent fever, severe abdominal pain, repeated vomiting, or other worsening symptoms also require professional medical assessment rather than repeated attempts to treat the problem with different medicines.

## Plan Ahead for a Safer and More Convenient Trip

To reduce the inconvenience of finding medicine at night, tourists can take several precautions before traveling:
- Bring the usual medicines you may need during your trip, while complying with applicable medicine-carrying regulations.
- Keep a record of generic medicine names, active ingredients, and allergy information.
- Identify pharmacies and medical facilities near your accommodation in advance.
- Save your hotel's contact information and local emergency numbers.
- Understand medicine storage requirements, especially for products that need refrigeration or special transportation conditions.

If you plan to carry medicines across international borders, check the relevant regulations of your destination and transit countries in advance.
