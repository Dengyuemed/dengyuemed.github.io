# What Are ADCs?

[Antibody-Drug Conjugates (ADCs)](https://dengyuemed.github.io/oncology/2026/07/17/adc-landscape-head-neck-cancer-her3-egfr-b7h3-emerging-targets/) are a type of targeted therapy that combines a monoclonal antibody with a highly potent cytotoxic drug (payload) through a specialized linker.

ADCs can be thought of as a **"precision delivery system."**

The treatment process generally includes four steps:

- The monoclonal antibody recognizes and binds to a specific target on the surface of cancer cells.
- The ADC is internalized into the cancer cell.
- Inside the cell, the linker is broken down.
- The cytotoxic payload is released to destroy the cancer cell while minimizing damage to healthy tissues whenever possible.

Because ADCs combine the precision of targeted therapy with the cancer-killing effects of chemotherapy, they are often referred to as **"precision chemotherapy."**

# How Do ADCs Treat Lung Cancer?

Many lung cancer cells express specific proteins that can serve as targets for ADC therapy.

Some of the most important biomarkers currently being explored include:

- HER2
- HER3
- TROP2
- DLL3 (primarily in Small Cell Lung Cancer)
- CEACAM5 (currently under investigation)

Each ADC targets a different biomarker, meaning **not every patient is suitable for the same ADC therapy.**

# Which Lung Cancer Patients May Benefit from ADC Therapy?

Eligibility depends on several factors, including lung cancer subtype, biomarker testing, previous treatment history, and approved indications.

## 1. HER2-Altered NSCLC

HER2 mutations occur in approximately **2%–4%** of patients with Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer.

HER2-targeted ADCs have created new treatment opportunities for patients whose disease progresses after conventional therapies.

Patients generally require genetic testing to confirm HER2 alterations before treatment eligibility can be assessed.

---

## 2. HER3-Related NSCLC

HER3 has become an increasingly important research target.

Several HER3-targeted ADCs are being developed for patients with EGFR-mutant [NSCLC](https://dengyuemed.github.io/oncology/2026/03/09/chinese-egfr-met-targeted-therapies-nsclc/) whose disease has progressed after EGFR-TKI therapy.

Multiple agents are currently undergoing clinical development or regulatory review.

---

## 3. TROP2-Positive NSCLC

TROP2 is highly expressed in various solid tumors.

TROP2-targeted ADCs have demonstrated encouraging anti-tumor activity in clinical studies involving lung cancer.

Treatment suitability depends on approved indications and each patient's clinical condition.

---

## 4. Small Cell Lung Cancer (SCLC)

Small Cell Lung Cancer remains an aggressive disease with limited treatment options.

ADCs targeting **DLL3** have become an important area of clinical research and may provide additional treatment opportunities for patients with relapsed or refractory [SCLC](https://dengyuemed.com/blog/5-key-types-of-lung-cancer/).

# Advantages of ADC Therapy

Compared with conventional chemotherapy, ADCs offer several potential advantages.

## More Precise Targeting

Monoclonal antibodies deliver anti-cancer drugs directly to tumor cells.

## Reduced Impact on Healthy Tissue

Because the payload is primarily released inside target cells, ADCs are designed to reduce damage to normal tissues.

However, systemic side effects may still occur and require monitoring.

## New Option After Drug Resistance

Patients whose disease progresses after targeted therapy or immunotherapy may have additional treatment opportunities through ADCs.

## Rapid Innovation

With more ADCs entering clinical trials and receiving regulatory approvals, lung cancer treatment options continue to expand.

# What Tests Are Needed Before ADC Therapy?

Before considering ADC treatment, physicians generally perform a comprehensive evaluation, including:

- Pathological diagnosis
- Lung cancer staging
- Genetic testing (EGFR, ALK, ROS1, HER2, etc.)
- Biomarker testing when appropriate
- Previous treatment history
- Liver function assessment
- Kidney function assessment
- Overall health evaluation

These assessments help determine whether ADC therapy is appropriate and support individualized treatment planning.
