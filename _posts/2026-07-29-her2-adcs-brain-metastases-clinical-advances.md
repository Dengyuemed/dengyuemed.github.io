In recent years, **[antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs)](https://dengyuemed.com/blog/adc-drugs-by-target-in-2026/)** have rapidly transformed the treatment landscape for HER2-positive solid tumors. By combining the precise targeting ability of monoclonal antibodies with highly potent cytotoxic payloads, HER2 ADCs have become an important therapeutic option for HER2-expressing cancers, including breast cancer and gastric cancer.

Brain metastases remain one of the greatest clinical challenges for patients with HER2-positive malignancies. Growing clinical evidence demonstrates that next-generation HER2 ADCs possess meaningful intracranial antitumor activity, making them one of the most promising therapeutic strategies for patients with central nervous system (CNS) involvement.

As a global pharmaceutical distributor, **DengYueMed** reviews several of the world's leading HER2 ADC therapies and summarizes the latest clinical evidence, current developments, and future trends in treating HER2-positive brain metastases.

# Why Have HER2 Brain Metastases Become a Key Focus of ADC Development?

[HER2-positive breast cancer](https://dengyuemed.com/product/breast-cancer/) is among the tumor types with the highest incidence of brain metastases. As anti-HER2 therapies continue to improve overall survival, brain metastases have become an increasingly important determinant of long-term patient outcomes.

Although conventional HER2-targeted monoclonal antibodies effectively control systemic disease, their ability to cross the **blood-brain barrier (BBB)** remains limited. Once brain metastases develop, however, the BBB is often partially disrupted, forming the **blood-tumor barrier (BTB)**, which may allow ADC molecules to reach intracranial tumor lesions more effectively.

In addition, next-generation HER2 ADCs feature several technological improvements, including:

- Higher drug-to-antibody ratios (DAR)
- Optimized linker technologies
- Potent cytotoxic payloads
- Strong bystander effects capable of eliminating neighboring tumor cells with heterogeneous HER2 expression

These advantages have made HER2 ADCs an increasingly important focus in brain metastasis research.

# Global Leading HER2 ADC Therapies

## 1. Trastuzumab Deruxtecan (T-DXd)

![Trastuzumab Deruxtecan (T-DXd)](/assets/images/trastuzumab-deruxtecan-tdxd.png)

[Trastuzumab Deruxtecan](https://dengyuemed.com/product/enhertu-trastuzumab-deruxtecan-her2-cancers/) (T-DXd) is currently regarded as one of the world's leading HER2 ADC therapies and has generated the strongest clinical evidence for treating HER2-positive brain metastases.

T-DXd incorporates several innovative design features:

- Cleavable linker
- High drug-to-antibody ratio (DAR)
- Topoisomerase I inhibitor payload
- Strong bystander effect

These characteristics enable T-DXd to demonstrate potent antitumor activity even in tumors with heterogeneous HER2 expression.

Multiple DESTINY clinical trials have consistently demonstrated:

- High intracranial objective response rates (ORR)
- Durable progression-free survival (PFS)
- Significant activity in HER2-positive brain metastases
- Clinical benefit in HER2-low breast cancer

Today, T-DXd is widely regarded as the global benchmark for HER2 ADC development.

## 2. Trastuzumab Emtansine (T-DM1)

![Trastuzumab Emtansine (T-DM1)](/assets/images/trastuzumab-emtansine-tdm1.png)

As the first approved HER2 antibody-drug conjugate, **[Trastuzumab Emtansine](https://dengyuemed.com/product/kadcyla-trastuzumab-emtansine-breast-cancer/) (T-DM1)** marked the beginning of the HER2 ADC era.

Although its molecular design differs from newer-generation ADCs, T-DM1 has accumulated extensive clinical experience in treating HER2-positive breast cancer and has demonstrated meaningful intracranial disease control in patients with brain metastases.

With the emergence of stronger clinical evidence supporting newer agents such as T-DXd, T-DM1 has gradually shifted from a frontline option to a subsequent-line treatment for selected patients. Nevertheless, its historical significance and clinical value remain substantial.

## 3. Disitamab Vedotin (RC48)

![Disitamab Vedotin (RC48)](/assets/images/disitamab-vedotin-rc48.png)

[Disitamab Vedotin](https://dengyuemed.com/product/aidixi-disitamab-vedotin/) (RC48), independently developed in China, has attracted increasing international attention in recent years.

Using **monomethyl auristatin E (MMAE)** as its cytotoxic payload, RC48 has demonstrated promising therapeutic potential in tumors with low-to-moderate HER2 expression. The drug has already received regulatory approval in China for multiple indications while continuing to advance its global clinical development.

Although clinical evidence regarding brain metastases is currently less extensive than that for T-DXd, ongoing clinical trials and real-world studies continue to evaluate its potential role in patients with HER2-positive brain metastases.

Its continued development reflects the rapid progress of China's innovative ADC ecosystem and its growing influence in the global oncology market.

## 4. ARX788

ARX788 is one of the most closely watched next-generation HER2 ADCs currently in clinical development. It utilizes **site-specific conjugation technology**, which is designed to improve molecular stability, optimize pharmacokinetic properties, and enhance the therapeutic index.

The drug is currently being investigated in multiple clinical studies involving:

- HER2-positive breast cancer
- Gastric cancer
- Other HER2-expressing solid tumors
- Patients with brain metastases

Although clinical evidence in brain metastases is still emerging, ARX788 continues to demonstrate encouraging antitumor activity and remains an important candidate in the next generation of HER2-targeted ADCs.

## 5. SHR-A1811

SHR-A1811 is another promising next-generation HER2 ADC developed in China.

Featuring a **topoisomerase I inhibitor payload**, SHR-A1811 follows a similar design philosophy to several internationally developed HER2 ADCs while incorporating proprietary optimization technologies.

Current clinical studies include patients with:

- HER2-positive breast cancer
- Gastric cancer
- Other HER2-expressing solid tumors

As China's ADC innovation ecosystem continues to mature, SHR-A1811 has become an increasingly competitive candidate in the rapidly evolving global HER2 ADC landscape.

## 6. DB-1303

DB-1303 is another emerging HER2 ADC that has attracted considerable international attention.

Its optimized linker and payload design aim to improve the balance between efficacy and safety while expanding treatment opportunities for HER2-positive malignancies.

Multiple international clinical trials are currently evaluating DB-1303 across a broad range of HER2-expressing solid tumors.

Although intracranial clinical evidence remains limited, continued global development positions DB-1303 as another promising candidate in the expanding HER2 ADC pipeline.

# Key Clinical Studies Driving the Development of HER2 ADCs

Several landmark clinical studies have accelerated the application of HER2 ADCs in the treatment of brain metastases.

Among the most influential are:

## DESTINY-Breast03

DESTINY-Breast03 demonstrated that **Trastuzumab Deruxtecan (T-DXd)** significantly improved progression-free survival compared with T-DM1 in patients with HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer while also showing encouraging activity in patients with brain metastases.

## DESTINY-Breast12

DESTINY-Breast12 further expanded the evidence supporting T-DXd in patients with active or stable brain metastases, reinforcing its role in controlling central nervous system disease.

## TUXEDO-1

The TUXEDO-1 study investigated T-DXd in patients with active HER2-positive brain metastases and reported encouraging intracranial responses, providing additional evidence supporting the use of ADCs in CNS disease.

## DEBBRAH

The DEBBRAH study explored the activity of T-DXd in patients with HER2-low breast cancer and brain metastases, further expanding the potential clinical application of HER2 ADC therapy.

Collectively, these studies suggest that next-generation HER2 ADCs can not only improve systemic disease control but also achieve clinically meaningful intracranial responses in selected patients.
