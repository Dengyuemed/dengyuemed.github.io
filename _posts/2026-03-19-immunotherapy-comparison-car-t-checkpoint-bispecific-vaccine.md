<h2>I. The Essence of Immunotherapy</h2>

<p>
Unlike chemotherapy and targeted therapy that directly kill tumor cells, immunotherapy works by activating or reshaping the body’s immune system to recognize and eliminate cancer.
</p>

<p>
These approaches act across different stages of the cancer immunity cycle:
</p>

<ul>
<li>Antigen release</li>
<li>Antigen presentation</li>
<li>T-cell activation</li>
<li>Tumor infiltration and killing</li>
<li>Immune memory formation</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>II. Mechanisms of Four Major Modalities</h2>

<h3>1. Checkpoint Inhibitors (ICI)</h3>

<p><strong>Mechanism:</strong> Block inhibitory pathways such as PD-1/PD-L1 and CTLA-4 to restore T-cell activity.</p>

<ul>
<li>Advantages: Broad applicability, first-line standard in multiple cancers</li>
<li>Limitations: Dependence on tumor microenvironment, immune-related adverse events</li>
<li>Trend: Combination therapies (PD-1 + LAG-3 / TIGIT)</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. CAR-T Cell Therapy</h3>

<p><strong>Mechanism:</strong> Genetically engineered T cells target tumor antigens independently of MHC.</p>

<ul>
<li>Advantages: High response rates, long-term remission potential</li>
<li>Limitations: Toxicity risks, high cost, limited solid tumor success</li>
<li>Trend: Dual-target CAR-T, in vivo CAR-T</li>
</ul>

<h3>3. Bispecific Antibodies</h3>

<p><strong>Mechanism:</strong> Simultaneously bind tumor cells and T cells to induce immune killing.</p>

<ul>
<li>Advantages: Off-the-shelf, precise targeting</li>
<li>Limitations: Cytokine release syndrome, continuous dosing</li>
<li>Trend: PD-1/VEGF and multi-target bispecifics</li>
</ul>

<h3>4. Tumor Vaccines</h3>

<p><strong>Mechanism:</strong> Present tumor antigens to activate immune response and memory.</p>

<ul>
<li>Advantages: Low toxicity, strong long-term potential</li>
<li>Limitations: Weak as monotherapy</li>
<li>Trend: Personalized mRNA vaccines + checkpoint inhibitors</li>
</ul>

<h2>IV. Clinical Strategy and Future Trends</h2>

<p>
The future of immunotherapy lies in combination strategies:
</p>

<ul>
<li>Checkpoint inhibitors + vaccines to activate “cold tumors”</li>
<li>CAR-T + bispecific antibodies to overcome resistance</li>
<li>Bispecific antibodies + ADCs for synergistic effects</li>
</ul>

<p>
Biomarker-driven decision-making using PD-L1, TMB, MSI, and ctDNA is becoming standard in precision oncology.
</p>
