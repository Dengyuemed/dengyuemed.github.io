## What Lung Cancer Treatment Options Are Available in China?

**Surgery**

For some patients with early-stage lung cancer, surgery is an important treatment option. Depending on tumor location, disease stage, and the patient's overall condition, doctors may consider procedures such as lobectomy or segmentectomy. Some major hospitals in China also perform video-assisted thoracoscopic surgery (VATS) and robot-assisted surgery.

**Radiation Therapy**

Radiation therapy can be used in the treatment of lung cancer at different stages. Common techniques include:
- Intensity-modulated radiation therapy (IMRT)
- Stereotactic body radiation therapy (SBRT)
- Image-guided radiation therapy (IGRT)

For some patients with early-stage non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who are not suitable candidates for surgery, SBRT may be a treatment option.

**Targeted Therapy**

China has access to many major lung cancer drugs, including targeted therapies used in the treatment of lung cancer. Domestic generic medicines and national drug price negotiations have also contributed to lower treatment costs for some therapies. Commonly used targeted medicines include osimertinib, gefitinib, erlotinib, afatinib, alectinib, and lorlatinib.

**Immunotherapy**

PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors are widely used for certain patients with lung cancer. Examples include pembrolizumab, nivolumab, and atezolizumab, as well as China-developed medicines such as sintilimab and toripalimab. In many cases, the cost of immunotherapy in China is generally lower than in Europe and the United States.

**Clinical Trials**

Lung cancer clinical research in China covers areas including novel targeted therapies, immunotherapy, and combination treatment approaches.

For eligible patients, clinical trials may provide additional treatment options. Whether a patient is suitable for a specific trial depends on the trial's inclusion criteria and an assessment by qualified medical professionals.

## Leading Hospitals for Lung Cancer Treatment in China

China has a wide range of lung cancer medical resources across different cities. International patients can explore hospitals based on their medical condition and treatment needs.

**Cancer Hospital, Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences — Beijing**

The Cancer Hospital of the Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences is part of the National Cancer Center system and has strong oncology expertise. Its multidisciplinary resources cover thoracic surgery, medical oncology, radiation oncology, pathology, and medical imaging, supporting comprehensive lung cancer diagnosis and treatment as well as a range of clinical research programs.

**Guangzhou Royallee Hospital — Guangzhou**

Guangzhou Royallee Hospital is a JCI-accredited international tertiary cancer hospital with resources in medical oncology, surgical oncology, radiation oncology, and related specialties. Its lung cancer services include chemotherapy, targeted therapy, immunotherapy, and other treatment approaches, with experience in serving international patients.

**Fudan University Shanghai Cancer Center — Shanghai**

Fudan University Shanghai Cancer Center is a major specialized cancer hospital in Shanghai. Its lung cancer-related resources include thoracic surgery, medical oncology, radiation oncology, pathology, and molecular diagnostics, supporting multidisciplinary assessment for patients with different treatment needs.

**Fuda Cancer Hospital — Guangzhou**

Fuda Cancer Hospital focuses on comprehensive cancer care and services for international patients. The hospital has JCI accreditation and provides oncology-related services including medical oncology, surgery, and various comprehensive treatment approaches.

**Peking Union Medical College Hospital — Beijing**

Peking Union Medical College Hospital is a major tertiary general hospital with extensive multidisciplinary medical resources. These resources can support lung cancer patients who have complex underlying conditions or require assessment and coordination across multiple medical specialties.

**Foresea Yihe International Medical Center — Guangzhou**

Foresea Yihe International Medical Center is an international medical service platform with an oncology center and a respiratory disease diagnosis and treatment center. It integrates multidisciplinary medical teams and provides comprehensive medical and long-term health management support.

## How Should International Patients Prepare for Lung Cancer Treatment in China?

For international patients considering lung cancer diagnosis or treatment in China, it is important not to make decisions about a hospital or treatment plan based solely on information found online. From preparing medical records and communicating with hospitals to arranging travel and follow-up care, several aspects need to be planned in advance.

First, patients should organize complete medical records, including pathology reports, imaging examinations, previous treatment records, and genetic testing results. These materials can help Chinese medical institutions better understand the patient's condition.

Patients can then communicate remotely with relevant medical institutions or undergo a case assessment to learn more about their areas of expertise, available medical resources, and whether they can provide services relevant to the patient's condition. Based on this information, patients can further discuss potential treatment options, hospitalization arrangements, costs, and international patient services.

In addition to treatment itself, international patients should also consider arrangements after cross-border medical care, such as: How long will I need to stay in China? How will follow-up care be arranged after I return home? How will medical information be shared between the Chinese hospital and my local doctors?

These are important considerations to plan before traveling to China for treatment. Because medical communication, medical-record preparation, and travel arrangements can involve multiple steps, some patients may also seek assistance from professional service organizations to coordinate these aspects.
